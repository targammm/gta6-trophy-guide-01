Roadmap to the Platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

  1. Stage 1 — Story playthroughChapters 1–4, the final mission and every named mission. 17 trophies. Do partner moments (Hot Together, Retail Therapy) while your partner is with you.
  2. Stage 2 — Strangers & contactsAndy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and 8 stranger strands. 6 trophies. Finish strands as they appear.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities & combatStunts, races, shooting range, drone races, fishing, bowling, heists and challenges. 19 trophies.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & explorationMeditation spots, cards, WAiNK spots, vehicles and all 52 areas of Leonida. 7 trophies.
  5. Stage 5 — Money, properties & 100%Marked Bills, shops, garages, Scooter Bros and A Long Road (100% completion) → Platinum. 6 trophies.

Missable = tied to a story moment or a one-shot situation. Do it when it appears, or replay the mission. Nothing else in this list can be missed — collectibles and activities stay open after the story.
